# Northfield Mining Co. Employee Policies (fictional sample)

This is a fictional document written to demonstrate the rag-doc-qa project.

## Working hours

The standard shift is 8 hours, with three shifts per day: 06:00–14:00, 14:00–22:00 and 22:00–06:00. Underground workers may not work more than 48 hours in a week.

Overtime must be approved in advance by the shift boss and is paid at 1.5 times the normal hourly rate. Work on a public holiday is paid at 2 times the normal rate.

## Leave

Full-time employees receive 18 days of paid annual leave per year. Up to 6 unused days can be carried over to the next year; any remaining days are lost.

Sick leave of up to 12 days per year is paid. A medical certificate is required for any absence longer than 2 consecutive days.

New parents receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave, which can be taken within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Training

Every new underground employee completes a 5-day induction course before starting work. Refresher safety training of 1 day is required every year.

Employees can apply for up to 40,000 rupees per year in education support for courses related to their job. Applications go to the HR department at least 30 days before the course starts.

## Reporting concerns

Any employee can report a safety or ethics concern anonymously through the confidential hotline. Reports are reviewed by the compliance officer within 5 working days. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is a dismissible offence.
