# Northfield Mining Co. Environmental Monitoring Plan (fictional sample)

This plan is a fictional document written to demonstrate the rag-doc-qa project. The limits below are illustrative and are not taken from any real permit.

## Water quality

Mine discharge water is sampled at three outfalls: OF-1 (tailings pond overflow), OF-2 (pit dewatering) and OF-3 (workshop drainage).

The permitted pH range for all outfalls is 6.5 to 8.5. Total suspended solids must stay below 100 mg/L as a monthly average. Dissolved iron must stay below 1.0 mg/L.

Outfall OF-1 is sampled daily. OF-2 and OF-3 are sampled weekly. If any sample exceeds a limit, the outfall is resampled within 24 hours and the environmental manager is informed the same day.

Two consecutive exceedances at the same outfall must be reported to the state pollution control board within 72 hours.

## Air quality

Ambient air is monitored at four stations on the lease boundary. PM10 is measured continuously and the 24-hour average limit is 100 micrograms per cubic metre. PM2.5 has a 24-hour limit of 60 micrograms per cubic metre.

When the PM10 hourly reading rises above 150 micrograms per cubic metre, water trucks are dispatched to haul roads within 30 minutes.

## Noise

Noise at the nearest village boundary must not exceed 55 dB(A) during the day and 45 dB(A) at night. Night is defined as 22:00 to 06:00. Blasting is not permitted at night.

## Data and reporting

All monitoring data is uploaded to the central database within 48 hours of collection. The environmental manager signs a monthly compliance report, which is submitted to the regulator by the 10th day of the following month.

Raw data is retained for seven years. Any correction to a submitted value must be logged with the reason, the original value and the name of the person who made the change.
